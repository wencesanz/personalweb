Wences Sanz-Alonso has spent more than 20 years close to the design industry, building the places where it meets, learns and finds work. Co-founder of Domestika, founder of MadinSpain and PlayRestart, curator of Paradís in València and, until recently, Creative Experiential Director at Magnific, where he led Upscale Conf in Málaga and San Francisco.
Along the way he has sat on many sides of the table: founding a strategic design studio for NTT Data, judging Cannes Young Lions and Awwwards, teaching at IE University and mapping 1,300+ independent studios in Just a Design List. What he enjoys most is connecting the right people, ideas and work.
